# Portfolio Phase 1.5 — Narrative map

**Editorial principle:** Reveal, don't explain.

| Destination | Visitor question | Narrative role | Content boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home | Who is behind this work, and where should I look next? | The quote creates an open first impression; a short verified introduction gives context; selected work and writing offer two paths deeper. | No explanation of the quote, long manifesto, invented personal anecdotes or three-pillar brand framework. |
| Work (/projects/) | What has been made, and what can it demonstrate? | Archive of 13 projects with clear status and evidence boundaries. | Historical links are not promoted to verified demos; INJAZ remains in development; Life-Advise remains in recovery. |
| Writing (/writing/) | What does this person think about and explain? | Curated archive of 17 distinct public posts, with Medium/DEV cross-posts deduplicated and original links retained. | No fabricated excerpts, metrics, article counts beyond the inspected profile, or duplicated cross-post entries. |
| About | What is useful to know about the person? | A few verified working interests and writing habits; leaves room for visitors to discover more through the work. | No invented hobbies, personal history, credentials, personality adjectives or exhaustive autobiography. |
| Engineering Approach | How are technical decisions approached? | A set of observed working habits framed as flexible, not a universal formula. | No claim that every project used an identical process. |
| Contact | Is there a natural way to start a conversation? | An invitation to discuss a project or an idea. | Avoid generic pressure-driven “hire me now” language. |
| Project case study | Why did this exist, what was done, and what is known now? | Context, problem, decisions, implementation, current evidence and reflection where the source supports it. | Omit unsupported sections rather than filling them with generic copy. |

## Cross-site voice

- Direct, conversational and practical.
- Explain the problem before the tool.
- Present trade-offs and personal conclusions as situated opinions.
- Use short marginal notes and metadata for rhythm, not decoration.
- Let occasional humour arise naturally; do not manufacture a “human” anecdote.

## Preservation and status rules

- Retain all 13 project records.
- Keep the legacy React app and root public assets unchanged.
- Do not represent INJAZ as deployed or Life-Advise as recovered.
- Do not alter production deployment settings.
